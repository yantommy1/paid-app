# 30-minute customer discovery script

Goal: learn what actually happens at this firm when an invoice ages past 30 days. Not "would you buy Paid". The goal is to discover what's true, not to validate the product.

## Before the call (5 min prep)

- Re-read their `01-targets.md` block
- Open their LinkedIn page in a tab — note 2 recent posts/projects to reference if rapport stalls
- Have the Pretium tracker spreadsheet open to log answers
- **Don't open Paid demo unless asked** — opening a demo turns a discovery call into a pitch and you'll learn nothing real

## Opening (3 min)

> Thanks for the time — really appreciate it. Quick context on how I want to use these 30 minutes: I'm going to ask you about your invoice and A/R workflow, mostly to learn where it breaks. I'll probably not show you Paid unless you ask, because the point of this call is for me to learn from you, not the other way around. Cool?

Wait for "cool." Don't proceed without explicit consent — it sets the tone that this is a conversation, not a pitch.

## A/R workflow probe (15 min)

Ask these in order. Each question is followed by **2–3 probes** depending on the answer. The goal is concrete stories, not generalities.

### Q1. Who at {firm} is responsible for invoicing and follow-ups?
- Probe: "Is that the same person for both, or split?"
- Probe: "What % of their week does the follow-up part eat?"
- Probe: "What was that person doing before this fell on their plate?"

### Q2. Walk me through what happened the last time a client paid you 60+ days late.
- Probe: "Who flagged it first — was it the bookkeeper, or did a principal notice?"
- Probe: "What did the email or call you sent look like?"
- Probe: "Did the client respond? How fast?"

### Q3. What tools touch your A/R right now?
- Probe: "QuickBooks Online or Desktop? Or something else?"
- Probe: "Where do you actually see what's overdue — QB report, spreadsheet, gut feel?"
- Probe: "Anything you've tried for collections that you stopped using? Why?"

### Q4. What's the dollar impact of late payments on the firm in a typical month?
- Probe: "Have you had to delay payroll or push back a hire?"
- Probe: "What's the worst write-off you've taken in the last 2 years?"

### Q5. If a magic wand made invoice follow-up 90% automatic — what would you NOT want it to do?
- This is the killer question. Listen for: "I'd never let an AI sound like me", "clients would hate it", "my brand depends on personal contact."
- These are the objections that kill the product if left unaddressed.

## "Show, don't pitch" (5 min)

Only if they ask "so what does Paid do?":
- Screen-share for 90 seconds max
- Show: aging table → click "draft reminder" → tone slider → preview the email → "send" goes to YOUR Gmail draft for review
- Stop. Don't show settings, don't show Stripe Connect, don't show the bookkeeper share
- The discovery call's job is to surface objections. The demo is the close call.

## Pricing test (3 min)

> If we charged $99/month flat — would that be a yes, a no, or a "tell me more"?

Listen for what they push back on. "Per user" people are different from "per firm" people. Capture verbatim.

Alternative test: "$2/invoice followed up" — see which mental model they snap to.

## Closing (3 min)

Hard ask: **"Would you be willing to pilot it for 30 days, free, in exchange for me texting you twice a week to ask how it's going?"**

If yes — you have a design partner. Schedule the install call right now.
If no — ask "what would have to be true for this to be a yes in 6 months?" and capture the answer.

## After the call (5 min — do this BEFORE the next meeting)

Log in the tracker spreadsheet:
- 3 verbatim quotes
- Their answer to Q1 (who owns A/R) and Q4 (dollar impact)
- Pricing reaction
- Their objection from Q5
- Yes/No/Maybe on the pilot ask

## What to NOT do on the call

- ❌ Don't talk for more than 30% of the time. They should talk 70%.
- ❌ Don't ask "would you use this?" — useless answer. Ask what they DO today.
- ❌ Don't argue with objections. Just write them down and ask "say more."
- ❌ Don't promise features on the call. "Interesting, let me think about whether that fits" is fine.
- ❌ Don't end with "let me follow up with a quote." End with the pilot ask or nothing.

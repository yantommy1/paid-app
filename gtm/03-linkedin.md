# LinkedIn — day-4 fallback

When the email gets no reply by end of day 4, switch channels. LinkedIn DMs to small-firm principals get ~3x the reply rate of cold email, but only if the connection request lands first.

## Sequence

**Day 4** — send connection request with note (300 char max)
**Day 4 + accepted** — send the DM within an hour of acceptance
**Day 8** — single follow-up DM if no reply

## Connection request note (under 300 chars)

> Hi {first_name} — fellow person who built something for boutique A/E billing pain. Curious if you'd swap notes on what actually breaks at {firm}. No pitch.

Character count: 156. Don't pad — short notes get accepted more.

## DM after acceptance

> Thanks for connecting. Quick context: I built Paid (paid-app.com) to handle invoice follow-ups for A/E firms — and the only way to make it not-bad is to actually talk to the principals who'd use it.
>
> Trying to chat with 10 firms in your size range. 25 min on Zoom, your questions about what we're building, mine about your A/R workflow. Worth a slot this week?

Word count: 71. Shorter than email because LinkedIn punishes long DMs.

## Day-8 bump

> {first_name} — last bump. If "talking to a stranger about invoicing" sounds awful, totally fair, just reply "pass" and I'll close the loop. Otherwise grab a slot here: {Calendly}.

Word count: 30.

## Pattern that works for engineers specifically

Engineers respond to specifics, not enthusiasm. So:
- ✅ "your team's on the 2024 Austin civil rotation"
- ✅ "I noticed your firm uses QuickBooks Online from your job posts"
- ❌ "love what you guys are doing!"
- ❌ "your firm is amazing"

## What to NOT do on LinkedIn

- Don't send voice messages — they get ignored
- Don't send video messages — same
- Don't post about Paid on your feed and then DM them — they'll think the DM is automated
- Don't pay for InMail — connection requests with notes outperform paid InMail 2:1 for this segment
